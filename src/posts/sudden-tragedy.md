---
title: "Sudden Tragedy"
date: 2026/05/29
page: "Trio"
---

Dolly passed away very suddenly today. She didn't have any ability to keep her hind legs tucked in and I took her to the emergency vet. She was just so skinny and didn't want to eat anything. I took her home, the vet found nothing. She had painkillers and some anti-inflammatories. By the end of the night, I think her little body gave up. She passed away in my arms.


I can't describe exactly what it's like when a little life fades away like that. She was warm and I felt her heart beat one moment, and then, slowly, she was gone.
